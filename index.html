Jaebeom Jeon

Integrated M.S.–Ph.D. student, Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Korea University

Computer architecture & system software

Korea University, Seoul

Hi, my name is Jaebeom Jeon, and I am in the integrated master’s and Ph.D. program at Korea University’s Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering. My primary research areas are computer architecture and system software, with a particular focus on GPU architecture. My current research interests include multi-chip module (MCM) GPU architectures and LLM serving systems, aiming to improve inference efficiency on GPU clusters.
